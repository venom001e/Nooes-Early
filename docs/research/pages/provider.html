Yes. Run the CLI with CMD_ZDR=1 (for example, CMD_ZDR=1 cmd) to enforce both zero data retention and no prompt training on every request across all plans. Some models don't have a ZDR-capable upstream yet; under CMD_ZDR=1 those requests fail rather than route through a non-ZDR provider. We're actively adding ZDR coverage to the remaining models. On many models CMD_ZDR=1 costs nothing extra (same price and plan usage); on others it moves the request to a ZDR-capable upstream that may charge more. Need ZDR on a specific model that doesn't have it today? Email support@commandcode.ai.
